Questions?

??
Does it cost anything?

Nope. Alpacafest is free. Please RSVP so we know how many families to expect.

Who is it for?

It's designed for children with special needs and their families, but everyone's welcome: siblings, parents, grandparents and caregivers. Include everyone in your party when you RSVP.

Is it sensory-friendly?

Yes. Events are planned with neurodivergent kids in mind, with space to take a break whenever you need one.

Will there be alpacas at every event?

Alpacas join us at many stops, and some events feature other friendly animals instead. Each event page will tell you which animals to expect. Either way, experienced handlers help every child say hello safely and at their own pace.

I don't see my city. Can you come to me?

More cities are on the way! Reach out using the "Let's Collab" section above and tell us where you are.

My city says "Notify me." What does that mean?

The date is locked in and we're finalizing the venue. Tap "Notify me" to send us a quick email and we'll let you know the moment registration opens.
